# Topic Packs: 60 Pre-Seeded Claims for Verification

Each entry includes: the common misconception, the likely reality direction, 
suggested primary source, and a difficulty rating for verification.

Format:
- **Misconception**: What people typically believe
- **Reality direction**: Which way the truth likely points
- **Key metric**: What to actually measure
- **Primary source**: Where to start
- **Verification difficulty**: Easy / Medium / Hard

---

## Pack 1: Global Poverty & Wealth

1. **World poverty is getting worse**
   - Misconception: More people live in extreme poverty than ever before
   - Reality direction: Extreme poverty has fallen dramatically (from ~36% in 1990 to ~9% in 2019)
   - Key metric: % living on <$2.15/day
   - Primary source: World Bank (SI.POV.DDAY) / Our World in Data
   - Difficulty: Easy ✓

2. **The world is divided into "rich" and "poor" countries**
   - Misconception: Countries are either developed or developing with nothing in between
   - Reality direction: Most countries are in the middle; the "gap" is largely gone
   - Key metric: Income distribution by country (GDP per capita PPP)
   - Primary source: Gapminder income data
   - Difficulty: Medium

3. **Foreign aid is the main driver of African economic growth**
   - Misconception: Africa depends on Western aid to survive
   - Reality direction: Aid is a tiny fraction of African GDP; domestic investment and remittances dominate
   - Key metric: Aid as % of GDP vs. FDI and domestic revenue
   - Primary source: World Bank, OECD DAC
   - Difficulty: Medium

4. **The richest 1% own half the world's wealth**
   - Reality direction: Actually true — but the framing matters (wealth ≠ income)
   - Key metric: Global wealth share by percentile
   - Primary source: Credit Suisse Global Wealth Report, World Inequality Database
   - Difficulty: Easy ✓ (but nuance is important)

5. **Most poor people live in poor countries**
   - Misconception: Poverty is concentrated in the world's poorest nations
   - Reality direction: Most of the world's poor now live in middle-income countries (India, Nigeria, etc.)
   - Key metric: Absolute number of people in poverty by country income classification
   - Primary source: World Bank / Andy Sumner research
   - Difficulty: Medium

6. **Economic growth requires environmental destruction**
   - Misconception: You can't grow GDP and reduce emissions simultaneously
   - Reality direction: Several countries have decoupled growth from emissions
   - Key metric: GDP growth vs. CO2 emissions trend (absolute decoupling)
   - Primary source: Our World in Data, Global Carbon Project
   - Difficulty: Hard (contested)

7. **China's poverty reduction is a myth**
   - Reality direction: China lifted ~800 million people out of extreme poverty 1990–2015
   - Key metric: China poverty headcount ratio over time
   - Primary source: World Bank
   - Difficulty: Easy ✓

8. **The global middle class is shrinking**
   - Reality direction: The global middle class is the fastest-growing income group
   - Key metric: % of world population in "middle" income bands
   - Primary source: Pew Research Center global income analysis
   - Difficulty: Easy ✓

## Pack 2: Health & Life Expectancy

9. **Life expectancy hasn't changed much**
   - Misconception: Humans have always lived about as long as they do now
   - Reality direction: Global life expectancy was ~35 in 1900, now ~73
   - Key metric: Global average life expectancy at birth over time
   - Primary source: Our World in Data / Gapminder
   - Difficulty: Easy ✓

10. **Child mortality is still extremely high everywhere**
    - Reality direction: Child mortality has fallen by ~60% since 1990 globally
    - Key metric: Under-5 mortality per 1,000 live births
    - Primary source: UNICEF / World Bank
    - Difficulty: Easy ✓

11. **AIDS is getting worse**
    - Reality direction: New HIV infections and AIDS deaths have fallen significantly since 2004 peak
    - Key metric: New HIV infections per year; AIDS-related deaths per year
    - Primary source: UNAIDS / WHO
    - Difficulty: Easy ✓

12. **Vaccines are losing effectiveness globally**
    - Reality direction: Vaccination coverage has increased substantially in developing countries
    - Key metric: DTP3 immunization coverage (%)
    - Primary source: WHO / World Bank (SH.IMM.IDPT)
    - Difficulty: Easy ✓

13. **Maternal mortality is unchanged in developing countries**
    - Reality direction: Global maternal mortality fell by ~45% between 1990 and 2016
    - Key metric: Maternal deaths per 100,000 live births
    - Primary source: WHO / World Bank (SH.STA.MMRT)
    - Difficulty: Easy ✓

14. **Cancer is the world's biggest killer**
    - Reality direction: Heart disease kills more; cancer is #2. But cancer rates are rising as other causes fall
    - Key metric: Deaths by cause globally (GBD study)
    - Primary source: WHO GHO / IHME Global Burden of Disease
    - Difficulty: Medium

15. **People in poor countries are sicker because of genetics**
    - Reality direction: Health outcomes are almost entirely explained by income, sanitation, and healthcare access
    - Key metric: Health outcomes controlling for income level
    - Primary source: Lancet / IHME GBD
    - Difficulty: Hard

16. **Suicide rates are rising everywhere**
    - Reality direction: Global suicide rate has fallen ~36% since 2000; varies hugely by region
    - Key metric: Age-standardized suicide rate per 100,000
    - Primary source: WHO (MH_12 indicator)
    - Difficulty: Medium

17. **Obesity is only a rich-country problem**
    - Reality direction: Obesity rates are rising fastest in middle-income countries
    - Key metric: % of adults with BMI >30 by country income group
    - Primary source: WHO NCD data / Our World in Data
    - Difficulty: Medium

## Pack 3: Education & Literacy

18. **Most of the world can't read**
    - Misconception: Global illiteracy is the norm
    - Reality direction: Global adult literacy rate is ~87%; was ~67% in 1980
    - Key metric: Adult literacy rate (%)
    - Primary source: World Bank (SE.ADT.LITR.ZS) / UNESCO
    - Difficulty: Easy ✓

19. **Girls don't go to school in developing countries**
    - Reality direction: Gender parity in primary school enrollment is near-achieved globally; secondary gaps are closing fast
    - Key metric: Gender Parity Index in primary and secondary school enrollment
    - Primary source: World Bank / UNESCO
    - Difficulty: Easy ✓

20. **Education quality is getting worse globally**
    - Reality direction: Mixed — enrollment up, but learning outcomes vary widely
    - Key metric: PISA scores vs. enrollment rates (need to separate access vs. quality)
    - Primary source: World Bank, PISA (OECD)
    - Difficulty: Hard (nuanced)

21. **University attendance is only for elites**
    - Reality direction: Tertiary enrollment has tripled since 1990 in developing regions
    - Key metric: Gross tertiary enrollment ratio
    - Primary source: UNESCO / World Bank
    - Difficulty: Easy ✓

22. **Children in poor countries rarely finish primary school**
    - Reality direction: Global primary completion rate is ~90%; biggest gaps are in Sub-Saharan Africa
    - Key metric: Primary school completion rate by region
    - Primary source: World Bank / UNICEF
    - Difficulty: Easy ✓

## Pack 4: Gender & Rights

23. **Women's representation in politics is unchanged**
    - Reality direction: Women's share of parliamentary seats has more than doubled since 1990 (from ~12% to ~27%)
    - Key metric: % of parliamentary seats held by women
    - Primary source: IPU Parline database / World Bank (SG.GEN.PARL.ZS)
    - Difficulty: Easy ✓

24. **Child marriage is as common as ever**
    - Reality direction: Child marriage prevalence has fallen significantly; rate dropped ~25% in 10 years in some regions
    - Key metric: % of women aged 20–24 married before age 18
    - Primary source: UNICEF
    - Difficulty: Easy ✓

25. **The gender pay gap is getting wider**
    - Reality direction: The raw gap has narrowed in most countries, though adjusted gaps remain
    - Key metric: Women's earnings as % of men's (raw and adjusted)
    - Primary source: ILO / OECD / World Economic Forum Gender Gap Report
    - Difficulty: Medium (framing matters a lot)

26. **Female genital mutilation is increasing**
    - Reality direction: Prevalence is declining, especially among younger cohorts
    - Key metric: % of girls 15–19 who have undergone FGM (youngest cohort as proxy)
    - Primary source: UNICEF / WHO
    - Difficulty: Medium

27. **Women in the Middle East and North Africa don't work**
    - Reality direction: Female labor force participation is low but rising in MENA; varies enormously by country
    - Key metric: Female labor force participation rate by country
    - Primary source: ILO / World Bank
    - Difficulty: Medium

## Pack 5: Climate & Environment

28. **Deforestation is accelerating everywhere**
    - Reality direction: Net deforestation is concentrated in tropics; many temperate regions are re-greening
    - Key metric: Net forest area change by region
    - Primary source: FAO Global Forest Resources Assessment / Our World in Data
    - Difficulty: Medium

29. **Renewable energy is too small to matter**
    - Reality direction: Renewables now account for ~30% of global electricity generation; fastest-growing energy source
    - Key metric: Share of electricity from renewables
    - Primary source: IEA / Our World in Data
    - Difficulty: Easy ✓

30. **Air quality is getting worse everywhere**
    - Reality direction: Outdoor air quality has improved in rich countries; still worsening in parts of South Asia
    - Key metric: PM2.5 concentration by country over time
    - Primary source: WHO / State of Global Air report
    - Difficulty: Medium

31. **Electric vehicles are a tiny niche**
    - Reality direction: EV share of new car sales exceeded 18% globally in 2023; much higher in China and Europe
    - Key metric: EV share of new passenger vehicle sales
    - Primary source: IEA Global EV Outlook
    - Difficulty: Easy ✓

32. **Nuclear power is the most dangerous energy source**
    - Reality direction: Deaths per TWh: coal ~25, oil ~18, nuclear ~0.07. Nuclear is statistically the safest.
    - Key metric: Deaths per unit of energy produced (including accidents)
    - Primary source: Our World in Data (citing Sovacool et al. / WHO)
    - Difficulty: Medium (contested framing)

33. **CO2 emissions are only a problem in rich countries**
    - Reality direction: China is now the world's largest emitter; rich countries are declining
    - Key metric: Total CO2 emissions and per-capita by country
    - Primary source: Global Carbon Project / Our World in Data
    - Difficulty: Easy ✓

## Pack 6: Crime & Safety

34. **The world is getting more violent**
    - Reality direction: Long-run decline in homicide rates; war deaths down from Cold War peaks (though recent uptick)
    - Key metric: Global homicide rate per 100,000; battle deaths over time
    - Primary source: UNODC / Our World in Data (Pinker-adjacent data, use carefully)
    - Difficulty: Hard (contested, politically sensitive)

35. **Terrorism is at an all-time high**
    - Reality direction: Global terrorism deaths peaked ~2014 (ISIS), have since fallen significantly
    - Key metric: Deaths from terrorism per year globally
    - Primary source: Global Terrorism Database (GTD) / Our World in Data
    - Difficulty: Medium

36. **More people are dying in wars than ever before**
    - Reality direction: War deaths peaked in WWII; post-Cold War era has been relatively peaceful, though Syria/Ukraine are significant
    - Key metric: Battle deaths per year / per 100,000
    - Primary source: Uppsala Conflict Data Program / Our World in Data
    - Difficulty: Medium

37. **Crime is rising in most cities**
    - Reality direction: Violent crime declined significantly in most Western cities 1990–2020; recent uptick in some places post-pandemic
    - Key metric: Violent crime rate per 100,000 (not absolute numbers)
    - Primary source: UNODC / national statistics (varies by country)
    - Difficulty: Hard (very country-specific)

38. **People are more likely to be killed by strangers**
    - Reality direction: Most homicides are committed by someone known to the victim; intimate partner violence is a leading cause
    - Key metric: % of homicide victims killed by acquaintance vs. stranger
    - Primary source: UNODC Global Study on Homicide
    - Difficulty: Easy ✓

## Pack 7: Population & Demographics

39. **World population is growing out of control**
    - Reality direction: Fertility rates are falling nearly everywhere; UN projects population to peak around 10–11 billion then stabilize
    - Key metric: Global total fertility rate over time; UN population projections
    - Primary source: UN DESA / Our World in Data
    - Difficulty: Easy ✓

40. **Africa's population explosion is inevitable**
    - Reality direction: African fertility rates are falling, faster in some countries than historical norms; education of girls is key lever
    - Key metric: Fertility rate trends in Sub-Saharan Africa by country
    - Primary source: UN DESA / Our World in Data
    - Difficulty: Medium

41. **Most migrants are fleeing to Europe**
    - Reality direction: Most migration is South-South (within developing regions); most refugees are hosted by developing countries
    - Key metric: % of refugees hosted by developing vs. developed countries
    - Primary source: UNHCR Global Trends report
    - Difficulty: Easy ✓

42. **Urbanisation is making poverty worse**
    - Reality direction: Urban poverty exists but rural poverty is deeper and more severe; urbanisation is historically associated with rising incomes
    - Key metric: Poverty rates urban vs. rural; historical urbanisation vs. income
    - Primary source: World Bank / Our World in Data
    - Difficulty: Medium

43. **Ageing populations are only a rich-country problem**
    - Reality direction: Ageing is happening faster in middle-income countries (China, Brazil, Thailand) than it did in Europe
    - Key metric: Median age and old-age dependency ratio by country income group
    - Primary source: UN DESA
    - Difficulty: Medium

44. **Immigration makes crime worse**
    - Reality direction: Most rigorous studies find immigrants commit crimes at lower rates than native-born citizens
    - Key metric: Crime rates by immigration status (multiple country studies)
    - Primary source: Cato Institute / academic studies (Ousey & Kubrin; NBER)
    - Difficulty: Hard (politically contested, varies by methodology)

45. **Most people now live in cities**
    - Reality direction: True as of ~2007; urban population crossed 50% globally. Now ~57% urban.
    - Key metric: % of global population in urban areas
    - Primary source: UN DESA / World Bank
    - Difficulty: Easy ✓
