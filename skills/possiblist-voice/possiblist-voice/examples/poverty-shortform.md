# Worked Example: Extreme Poverty → Shortform Teaser

**Input**: Verified Fact Card — Global Extreme Poverty  
**Output format**: Shortform teaser, 120–180 words  
**Purpose**: Quiz lead-in — appears before the quiz question, creates curiosity, withholds answer

---

## The Wrong Kind of Wrong

On a small planet in an unremarkable corner of the galaxy, eight billion beings with
access to five thousand years of recorded history and an increasing array of data-
gathering technology have, in recent surveys, managed to answer a basic factual question
about their own world with a success rate approximately equivalent to — and in some
cases marginally worse than — selecting an answer at random.

The question concerned extreme poverty. Most people, asked whether it has got better or
worse over the past thirty years, said worse. Quite a lot of people said it had stayed
the same. The fraction who got it right was, statistically speaking, unimpressive.

The world has, in this respect, been doing something rather different from what most
of its inhabitants believe.

**What do you think has happened to global extreme poverty since 1990?**
