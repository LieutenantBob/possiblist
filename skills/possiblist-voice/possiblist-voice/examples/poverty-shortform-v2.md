## The Wrong Kind of Wrong

Somewhere in the quieter reaches of the galaxy, a species that has constructed
elaborate systems for measuring almost everything has maintained, with impressive
consistency, a comprehensive misunderstanding of how one of the most important things
is going.

I should say that I had this misunderstanding too. Asked whether global extreme poverty
was getting better or worse, I would have said worse. Most people would — and do.
Fifty-two percent of respondents in surveys across multiple countries said worse.
Roughly another quarter said about the same.

Nine percent got it right. Which is, depending on how you feel about random chance,
either reassuring or slightly bracing.

The world has, in this particular respect, been doing something rather different from
what most of its inhabitants believe.

**What do you think has happened to global extreme poverty since 1990?**
