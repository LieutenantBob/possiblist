## Notes on Contact Approach

1. **X handles above may contain placeholder characters** due to content filter considerations. Verify each handle before outreach.
2. **Personalise every outreach.** Reference their specific work. "I read [specific article/book] and it made me think you'd resonate with this."
3. **Keep initial contact short.** One paragraph, one link (the manifesto or the quiz), one clear ask.
4. **The AI benchmark is the news hook.** For journalists and podcast bookers, lead with "we ran original research" not "we built a movement."
5. **The manifesto is the identity hook.** For authors and intellectuals, lead with "we wrote something we think you'd recognise yourself in."
6. **Gapminder first.** Before any public launch, the Rosling family and Gapminder should be contacted. Their response shapes everything else.
